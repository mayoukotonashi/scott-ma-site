Dissertation

A Confucian Science

How did agricultural experts gain the authority to tell farmers how to live? My dissertation follows ideas about the role of agricultural expertise in good government that emerged among Confucian scholars in the eighteenth century, became ensconced within the agricultural colleges of imperial Japan, were extended to the countryside of the empire's colonies and, eventually, established the groundwork of Japanese foreign aid.

2020–2023

The politics of culture in Japan

My earlier research studied the politics and discourse of culture in contemporary Japan. I wrote on varied topics including college women's fashion; overseas Japan expositions; immigrant communities; and pop fiction. I show how globalization and affluence in the last half century have marked a fundamental shift in how the Japanese conceptualize their own culture; how it relates to foreign ones; and the relationship between culture, politics, and the state.
